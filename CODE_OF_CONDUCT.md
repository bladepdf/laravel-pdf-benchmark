# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on the work, and respect differing experience and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or other private contact method published for the project. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
